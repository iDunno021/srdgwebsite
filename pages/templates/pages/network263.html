SRDG is a proud supporter and partner of Network 263 —
committed to advancing sustainable development and
empowering young people across Harare, Zimbabwe through
education, entrepreneurship and long-term self-sufficiency.

THE WHY

Two organisations, one belief: that meaningful opportunity
changes everything. Together we're building a pathway
from education to self-sufficiency — for individuals and
whole communities.
